Rules & Settings

Tune the initial stock status logic. These are business rules, not AI predictions.
Mixed MT formats are normalized into dated stock snapshots. Unknown sales periods are retained but not used to calculate Days of Stock. Snapshot duplicates are replaced by MT chain + branch/report group + SKU + report date; earlier dates remain in history. Records without a branch are flagged as report-level, not branch-level.

Stock Intelligence data model & AI roadmap

Normalized stock snapshot

One record per MT chain + branch/report group + SKU + report date. Store source file, import time, opening stock, stock-in, stock-out/sales quantity, closing stock, sales period days, and branch-level flag. Re-uploading the same snapshot key replaces that record; other dates remain as history.

Upload controls

Detect columns across chain-specific layouts, preview mapped records, exclude total rows, flag missing branch names, capture the report date, and validate stock arithmetic when the required movement fields exist. Unknown sales periods remain unknown rather than being treated as daily sales.

Stock status rules

Stockout: closing stock ≤ 0. Replenishment Needed: known days of stock ≤ supplier lead time. Low Stock: known days of stock ≤ low-cover threshold. High Stock: known days of stock ≥ high-cover threshold. Without a valid sales period, non-zero stock is marked Sales Data Needed.

AI roadmap

Phase 1: dependable imports and historical snapshots. Phase 2: compute average daily sales only from validated periods and compare successive snapshots. Phase 3: forecast branch-SKU demand using several weeks/months of history, seasonality and promotions. Phase 4: prioritize risks and suggest order quantities using lead time, safety stock, incoming stock and case-size constraints. Recommendations remain decision support until reviewed by the MT team.

Sales & Stock Coverage

Sales history loaded from your workbook. NSI is treated as sales value; TotalQuantity is used for unit-based stock cover.
Coverage methodology: HQ stock units ÷ average monthly TotalQuantity, using the inclusive month span available for each MT chain. NSI is displayed separately as sales value. The Stock Tracker uses chain-level product sales as a clearly labelled proxy against branch stock; this is not branch-specific sales coverage. NSI is shown separately as sales value and is not divided into unit stock.
